Draft outline, to be written by me (not published until `draft` is removed):

- The problem: releases depended on a few people remembering the steps.
- What the bot did: build, deploy and release from chat, with the result posted back where everyone could see it.
- What mattered more than the bot: one visible log of who released what, and steps that were safe to run twice.
- What carried over to AI agents: the same rules about permissions, visibility and safe retries.
